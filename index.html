BinTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

BinTrack ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our BinTrack mobile application.

Information We Collect

BinTrack collects the following information:

Account Information: If you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique user identifier and, optionally, your name and email address.

Inventory Data: Items and bins you create are stored locally on your device. If you subscribe to BinTrack Pro, this data is synced to Firebase Firestore to enable cross-device access.

Usage Data: We use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the app.

Advertising: Free users see ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve BinTrack, provide cloud sync for Pro subscribers, display relevant advertisements to free users, and send low stock notifications you have enabled.

Data Storage

Local data is stored on your device using standard iOS storage. Pro subscriber data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore. We do not sell your data to third parties.

In-App Purchases

BinTrack offers in-app purchases including Remove Ads ($1.99) and BinTrack Pro ($4.99). All purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

Third Party Services

BinTrack uses the following third party services: Google Firebase (authentication and cloud storage), Google AdMob (advertising), and Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases).

Children's Privacy

BinTrack is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You can disable notifications in your iPhone Settings. You can opt out of personalised ads in your iPhone Settings under Privacy.
